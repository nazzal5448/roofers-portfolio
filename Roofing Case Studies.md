# Case Study 1:

## **Client: Confidential**

## **Business: Windsor Roofing Solutions**

## **Location: Birmingham, United Kingdom**

---

## **🎯 Goal**

* Increase rankings for roofing service keywords in Birmingham  
* Grow organic traffic and inbound roofing leads  
* Improve visibility across Google and AI search

---

## **⚙️ Actions Taken**

* Rebuilt website targeting high-intent roofing keywords  
* Fixed on-page SEO (content, titles, structure, internal linking)  
* Improved technical SEO and site performance  
* Built relevant backlinks and strengthened domain authority  
* Optimized service pages for better local search visibility  
* Implemented AEO strategy to improve AI search presence

---

## **📈 Results**

* Organic traffic: **\~1.1K/month (+147%)**  
* Traffic share: **66% in market**  
* 250+ keywords ranking  
* Authority score improved to **11**  
* AI visibility: **0 → 22** (10 mentions, 2 cited pages)  
* Consistent growth in inbound roofing inquiries

---

## **⏱️ Duration**

* \~4–5 months

---

## **💰 Budget**

* \~$500/month

---

## **✅ Key Outcome**

* Scaled organic visibility and traffic significantly, generating consistent roofing leads in a competitive Birmingham market
